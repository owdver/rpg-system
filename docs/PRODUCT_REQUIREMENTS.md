# Product Requirements

## Product Objective
Build a premium fitness operating system for users who want intelligent guidance, adaptive training, and immersive progression tied to physical capability. The product must feel like a living system that evaluates the user and continuously develops their performance profile.

## Primary User Outcomes
- Improve consistency and adherence to training.
- Increase trust in the system through clear and meaningful feedback.
- Create a sense of personal evolution through XP, levels, ranks, titles, achievements, and skills.
- Reduce friction through offline-first workflows and seamless health integration.

## Functional Requirements
1. Capture workout intent, execution, recovery, and readiness from user actions and connected health sources.
2. Generate dynamic missions, adaptive recommendations, and evaluation reports.
3. Track and display progress across XP, levels, ranks, titles, achievements, daily missions, weekly missions, and seasonal challenges.
4. Provide immersive screens for home, training HUD, recovery, performance overview, inventory, skill tree, and settings.
5. Integrate with Health Connect on Android and Apple HealthKit on iOS.
6. Support offline-first operation with local storage, queued sync, and conflict resolution.
7. Deliver event-driven feedback through motion, sound, haptics, and contextual system messages.
8. Support secure authentication, data privacy controls, and user-managed permission consent.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Target 120 FPS animations on supported devices.
- Use hardware acceleration and efficient particle systems.
- Minimize rebuilds and avoid expensive layout thrash.
- Maintain battery-conscious rendering and background behavior.
- Provide accessible color contrast, readable typography, reduced-motion support, and screen-reader compatibility.
- Ensure secure storage and privacy-aware handling of health and biometric data.

## Success Metrics
- Daily active usage.
- Session completion rate.
- Mission adherence and recovery consistency.
- 30-day and 90-day retention.
- User satisfaction with recommendation quality and immersion.
