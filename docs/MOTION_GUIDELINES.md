# Motion Guidelines

## Motion Philosophy
No screen change should simply fade. Every transition should feel like a system event: projection, scan, pulse, materialization, or energy sweep.

## Required Motion Patterns
- Window materialization from a scanline field.
- Stat increase with count-up, pulse, and glow.
- XP overflow animation into the next level.
- Mission completion with shockwave and particle burst.
- Recovery warning with orbital pulses and diagnostic overlays.

## Motion Parameters
- Duration: 180ms to 900ms.
- Curves: spring, ease-out, and custom energy curves.
- Triggers: tap, state change, mission events, and system alerts.
